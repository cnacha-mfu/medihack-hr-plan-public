# What You Must Deliver — MediHack

**Everything below is due 18 September 2026.** The final demo is on 27 September; the week in between is for your advisor to review your submission and for you to act on the feedback. Submitting on 18 Sep is the deadline, not a target — work handed in later cannot be reviewed before the demo.

1. **Working prototype** — one per team, running end to end from a clean Git clone, with demo data included. No credentials or patient data in the repo.
2. **Docs in the repo** — setup guide, user guide **in Thai** with screenshots, architecture diagram, API reference.
3. **Evaluation evidence** — your test dataset and raw results file. Every number in the report must trace back to it.
4. **Final report, 25–40 pages** — Ch.1 Problem: the hospital problem in the hospital's terms · Ch.2 Related work: existing approaches and why you chose your technology · Ch.3 Design: workflow before/after, architecture, data model, wireframes · Ch.4 Implementation: how it works, the AI part in detail (prompts, context, safeguards) · Ch.5 Data & PDPA: what data you used, how it was de-identified, stored, disposed of · Ch.6 Testing & evaluation · Ch.7 Results against your objectives · Ch.8 Conclusion and what is needed for real deployment · Appendices: API, schema, test cases, questionnaires, screenshots.
5. **Chapter 6 is the one that matters.** It must prove **TRL 4**: the system tested as a whole, on realistic data, with measured numbers. Include your **test cases and their pass/fail results** covering the critical paths, **performance testing where it applies** (response time, throughput, load, AI output accuracy), a comparison against the hospital's current process, feedback from medical staff, and an honest limitations section.
